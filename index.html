About Temple

Palnadu Yadadri Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy Vaari Devastanam is a sacred temple dedicated to Lord Narasimha Swamy, located in the serene village of Jangamaheswarapuram, Gurazala. The temple attracts devotees from across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, known for its divine energy and traditional architecture inspired by Yadadri.
