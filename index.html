About Temple

Palnadu Yadadri Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy Vaari Devastanam is a sacred temple dedicated to Lord Narasimha Swamy, located in the serene village of Jangamaheswarapuram, Gurazala. The temple attracts devotees from across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, known for its divine energy and traditional architecture inspired by Yadadri.

Temple Events & Timings

Every Saturday:   Special Puja and Alamkaram

Temple Timings:   Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy Vari Temple welcomes devotees daily from
                               5:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM.

On special occasions and auspicious days, temple timings may be extended or adjusted to accommodate special poojas and rituals. All arrangements are lovingly overseen by the Temple Management Committee.
